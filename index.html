Findings from the College-town home sales dataset

This site looks at home sales near university campuses. Each row in the dataset is one completed sale, with the dates the home was listed, went under contract and closed. The figures are as the source reported them and have not been checked against county records.

    How these numbers were calculated

    • Every figure is computed from data/college_town_home_sales.csv when the page loads.
    • The median sale price is the middle sale price of one city's home sales, after sales under $10,000 are left out. That cutoff leaves out – sales.
    • These are prices paid to buy homes. They are not rents, so they do not show what a student would pay to live in a home.
    • The sales were collected in March 2026 from public property listings. The prices are as the source reported them and have not been checked against county records.

    Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
